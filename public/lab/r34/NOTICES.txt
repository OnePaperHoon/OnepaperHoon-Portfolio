## R34 fork: vehicle model and media

This fork replaces the bundled VEYRA concept media with renders of a Nissan Skyline GT-R R34.

- 3D model: "2002 Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 V-Spec II" by supercarmodels, <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2002-nissan-skyline-gt-r-r34-v-spec-ii-385220e90764403daaa1823296d40b54>, licensed CC-BY-4.0 according to the metadata embedded in the file. The copy used was retrieved from a public GitHub mirror (`AntoMillanBArcelo/TFG_Tunig`, `public/models/`), SHA-256 `bbe498c023f3ed6563d64e64a235ea9b65759a4131ff60297d97aae8dbb88f4a`. The model's own provenance has not been independently verified; check it before any public or commercial use.
- Changes made to the model for rendering: hood separated so it can open, body paint, glass and wheel-finish materials replaced, the low-polygon engine baked into the engine bay removed, and a procedurally modelled engine, gearbox, transfer case, propeller shafts, differentials and half shafts added (`production/r34/powertrain.py`). Those parts are an original illustrative reconstruction, not Nissan geometry.
- All stills and the four hover clips in `public/media/` were rendered offline with Blender Cycles by `production/r34/render.py` and exported by `production/r34/post.sh`. No generative image or video service was used.
